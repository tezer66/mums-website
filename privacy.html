Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

HarperHolistic is run by Gillian Harper (“Gill”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect when you use harperholistic.xyz, why we collect it and how we look after it. We treat what you share with care and respect.

What we collect

  • Your account: your first name, email address and password. Your password is stored securely by our membership provider, and we can never see it.
  • Your plan and payments: the plan and deck you chose and your payment history. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach this website or us.
  • What you create on the site: the cards you draw each day and your journal entries.
  • “Tell Gillian about you”: if you choose to answer, this includes what you’d like to be called, your birthday, your country, where you are in your healing, what’s on your heart, what makes you happy and how you’d like to be contacted. These answers can be personal, so every question except the consent box is optional.
  • On your device: the site saves a copy of your cards and journal in your browser so it works quickly. Our membership provider uses a small file (a cookie or local storage) to keep you signed in. We don’t use advertising cookies or tracking for advertising.

How we use it

  • To create your account, take payment and give you access to your decks.
  • To save your daily cards and journal, so you can see them on any device.
  • So Gill can read your answers and write you personal messages, if you tick the box to agree.
  • To email you about your membership, such as receipts, password resets and important changes.

We never sell your information, and we never share it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Gill can see your name, email, plan and your answers to “Tell Gillian about you”.
  • Your journal is stored with your account so it follows you to any device. Gill can technically access account records, but she does not read members’ journals. The only exception is if you ask her to, for example to help with a problem.
  • Service providers store and process information for us:
    • Memberstack (accounts and membership data)
    • Stripe (payments)
    • GitHub Pages (hosting the website)
    • Google Fonts (the website’s fonts)
    They may store information outside your country, including in the United States.
  • We may share information if the law requires it.

Keeping it safe and for how long

Information is sent over a secure connection and stored by our providers, who protect it with industry-standard security. We keep your information while you are a member. After you leave, we delete your account information within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your answers to Gill at any time from Edit your answers on your dashboard.
  • You can download or delete journal entries from your journal.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. To do any of these, email us.
  • If you’re unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. In Australia, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

HarperHolistic is for adults. Please don’t join if you are under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will let you know by email or on the website.

Greetings beautiful soul,

The full moon is a time to pause, look back and let go.

Before you reach for a crystal or your journal, think about the one sentence you have been holding back.

Write the boundary first, then the release

Full moon rituals are usually framed as letting go. I think they’re really about saying no.

Releasing a habit, a grudge or a role only sticks when you have already set the boundary that protects the space you’ve cleared. That’s why my Boundaries deck sits so naturally beside the full moon. It’s the least mystical thing I make, and maybe the most spiritual: it’s simply about saying no, kindly and clearly.

Your full moon ritual

1.  Sit quietly under the moon, or by a window, to ground yourself.

2.  Write the boundary first, one sentence beginning “I am allowed to…” or “I will no longer…”

3.  Then write what you are releasing.

4.  Shuffle the deck, draw your card and say the affirmation out loud.

Silence is the story

Cancer is silent until it screams. When I was diagnosed with Stage 4 cancer, I learned that the boundary you won’t say can show up in your body as a symptom you can’t ignore.

That is my lived experience, not a medical rule. Please always follow your medical team.

But it turns a spiritual idea into a question you can actually answer: what have I been swallowing?

Nobody tells the caregiver to rest

The person least likely to have their boundaries respected is often the one sitting beside someone who is ill.

A national survey of about 200,000 adults, published in Supportive Care in Cancer, found that people caring for someone with cancer had 89% higher odds of frequent mental distress than people who weren’t caregivers (it shows a link, not a cause; read the summary). And in October, the NCCN, an alliance of leading US cancer centres, published its first free guide written for people caring for someone with cancer.

Most cancer support speaks to the person with the diagnosis. But often the one at the bedside needs the words even more. So, gently: rest. You matter too.

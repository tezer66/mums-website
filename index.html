Before you join

Questions & answers

What do I get as a member?

All three decks: Boundaries are my Love Language, Cancer Care Cards and Momma’s Earth Magick, 44 cards each. You choose a daily card, write in your private journal, look back on your Weekly Reflections, read the guidebook, and shuffle as often as you like in Inner Play. You can also choose to receive Gill’s monthly newsletter.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

You add your card when you join, but nothing is charged for 7 days. During the trial you choose your daily card on every deck and write in your journal. On day 8 your membership begins on the plan you chose, and the full decks, the guidebook and Inner Play open up. Cancel before then and you won’t be charged at all.

How do I cancel?

Email gill@harperholistic.xyz any time and Gill will cancel it and confirm by email. There’s no lock-in contract. Your decks stay open until the end of the month or year you’ve already paid for.

Monthly or yearly?

Both include everything. Monthly is US$9.99 a month. Yearly is US$59 a year, which saves 51%, and US$5 of every yearly membership goes to cancer research.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer, and your cards and journal are the same on all of them. iPhone and Android apps are on their way.

Is my journal private?

Yes. What you write is saved to your own account, so only you can read it, on any of your devices. Our Privacy Policy explains how your information is looked after.

Are the cards advice?

The cards are for inspiration, reflection and comfort. They aren’t medical or psychological advice, and they don’t replace care from a doctor or counsellor.
